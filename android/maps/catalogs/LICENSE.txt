GameMap original navigation icon artwork

All SVG geometry in this directory was created specifically for this project.
It contains no copied game artwork, logos, sprites, emoji, fonts or external images.
The theme names describe visual inspiration only. No affiliation or endorsement
by the owners of any game franchise is claimed.

To the extent possible under law, the contributors dedicate this original
navigation artwork to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal.
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The original NavSkin art is editable SVG. Its raster PNGs are derived from those
SVG files. The explicit rasterSource for gta/vehicles/arrow takes precedence
over its legacy NavSkin SVG when preparing the runtime PNGs.

Exception: gta/vehicles/arrow-original.png is the original GTA San Andreas
radar_centre sprite exported from models/hud.txd. Copyright Rockstar Games.
Its source and the PNG derivatives gta/vehicles/arrow.png and arrow@2x.png
are excluded from the CC0 dedication. No outside-game redistribution license
has been verified; keep these resources in the local private asset pack.
Source, exact SHA-256 and the pixel-preserving transformation are recorded in
catalog.json and the generated provenance.json. Other vehicle choices retain
the original NavSkin geometry and their semantic IDs.

No Maki geometry is included in this catalog. Existing POI icons elsewhere in
the repository keep their own licenses and attribution.
